How a pick is judged
  1. Better than the market? Log-loss against the buyable price, game by game; a positive gap means the model is closer to the truth.
  2. Before the market? Closing line value: did the price move toward our pick before the game started?
  3. Money after fees? Profit per contract on settled paper bets, fees included, with a 95% interval.

Glossary
Log-loss
A score for probability forecasts: confident wrong answers cost a lot. Lower is better.
Brier score
Average squared gap between the forecast and what happened. Lower is better.
Paired difference
Market log-loss minus model log-loss on the same games. Positive means the model was closer.
CLV
Closing line value, in cents: the closing price minus the price we logged. A leading indicator, not a gate.
Fee
Kalshi's trading fee, taken out of every paper profit figure on this page.
Paper only. No real money.
